# Bluebird Bakery — bread, pastry and coffee in Lincoln Square

Source URL: https://bluebird-bakery.example/
Recorded on: 2026-09-14

Bluebird Bakery is a small neighborhood bakery and coffee counter in the Lincoln Square part of north
Chicago. We bake everything we sell in the shop, every morning, and when it is gone it is gone. There is
one Bluebird. There has only ever been one Bluebird, and we like it that way.

## Hours

Wednesday to Sunday, 7am to 2pm, or until the case is empty. On a good Saturday that is closer to
noon. Closed Monday and Tuesday. Closed the first week of January and the week of the Fourth of July,
because the two of us need a holiday too.

## Where to find us

15 Example Street, Chicago, IL 60625, in the blue building with the striped awning, two doors down from
the hardware store. Street parking out front, and a bike rack that fits six. The shop seats fourteen
inside and six at the two tables on the sidewalk. It is small. On Saturday there is a line, and the
line moves.

Phone: 312-555-0106. Somebody picks up during shop hours. Outside shop hours the phone rings in an
empty kitchen, so please try again in the morning.

## What we bake

**Bread.** A naturally leavened country loaf, a seeded rye, and a soft milk bread. The country loaf
uses a starter that came out of a jar in a friend's fridge in 2016 and has been fed every day since.
Bread comes out of the oven at about 8am. We bake roughly sixty loaves a day and we sell out most days.

**Pastry.** Croissants, pain au chocolat, a morning bun rolled in cardamom sugar, and a kouign-amann
on Saturday and Sunday only. Laminated dough is made over three days, so what you eat on Sunday was
started on Thursday.

**Sweets.** One cake by the slice that changes every week, brownies, and whatever fruit is good that
week turned into a galette. In the summer that means peaches from Fredericksburg. In the winter it
means citrus.

**Savory.** One quiche and one ham-and-cheese croissant daily. That is the whole savory menu and we
are not planning to grow it.

**Coffee.** Espresso and filter coffee from a roaster two miles from here. Drip, cortado, latte, and
iced versions of all three in the summer, which in Chicago means March to November.

## Whole cakes and big bread orders

We make whole cakes for birthdays and small celebrations, and we will set aside up to ten loaves for a
dinner or a party. How that works, how much notice we need and what it costs is written up on the
[Cakes and orders](https://bluebird-bakery.example/order) page. The short version: it starts with a
conversation with one of us, not with a form.

## Who we are

Bluebird is Hannah and Pete Kowalski. Hannah bakes. Pete runs the counter, keeps the books, and rolls
croissants on Friday nights when the weekend is going to be busy. There is more about how we ended up
here on the [About](https://bluebird-bakery.example/about) page.

## A note on prices

We raised prices in March 2026 for the first time in two years, by about eight percent, because flour
and butter went up and we would rather say so than quietly make the croissants smaller.
