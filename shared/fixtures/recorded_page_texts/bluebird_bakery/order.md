# Cakes and orders — Bluebird Bakery

Source URL: https://bluebird-bakery.example/order
Recorded on: 2026-09-14

Most of what we sell, you simply walk in and buy. This page is for the two things that need planning:
whole cakes, and bread in quantity.

## How to order

To order a cake, call us or stop by the counter. We do not take orders online.

That is not an oversight. There is no order form on this site, no shopping cart, no app, and we are not
listed on any delivery service. If a website other than this one offers to sell you a Bluebird cake, it
is not us. Every order we take starts with Hannah or Pete writing it by hand in the order book that
lives next to the register.

- **By phone:** 312-555-0106, Wednesday to Sunday, 7am to 2pm. If the line is long we may ask to call
  you back after the rush, and we will.
- **In person:** come to the counter at 15 Example Street, Chicago, IL 60625. Mid-morning on a
  Wednesday or Thursday is the calm time to talk through a cake.

Please do not send cake orders by email or by direct message. We read both late and badly, and an order
we did not see is a birthday without a cake. We will only count an order as placed once one of us has
said it back to you and written it in the book.

## Whole cakes

We make one size of round cake, eight inches, which feeds ten to twelve people, and a half sheet that
feeds about thirty. Flavors follow the season:

- Vanilla bean with strawberry jam and buttercream
- Chocolate with espresso buttercream
- Lemon and olive oil with whipped mascarpone
- Carrot with cream cheese frosting and toasted pecans
- In summer, peach and brown butter. In winter, orange and almond.

An eight-inch cake is $58. A half sheet is $120. A short message piped on top is included. We do not do
fondant, sculpted cakes or tiered wedding cakes, and we will happily tell you who in Chicago does.

**Notice:** seven days for a whole cake. In May, June and December, make it two weeks. We cap cakes at
six a week, because the same two ovens bake the bread.

## Bread in quantity

We will set aside up to ten loaves of the country loaf, the seeded rye or the milk bread for a dinner, a
party or a very large sandwich. Three days' notice. Same way to order: call us or stop by.

We do not do wholesale. We do not supply cafes, restaurants, grocers or offices. A bakery our size that
starts a wholesale account stops being able to bake well for the people who walk in.

## Paying and picking up

You pay when you pick up, at the register, by card or cash. We do not take deposits and we do not take
payment over the phone. Pickup is at our one shop in Lincoln Square during shop hours; we do not deliver.
If your plans change, call us at least two days ahead and there is no charge.

## Why we do it this way

Honestly, because there are two of us. Hannah Kowalski bakes and Pete Kowalski runs the counter, and
neither of us has found the evening to set up anything cleverer than a phone and a notebook. People
ask most weeks whether they can order a cake from their couch at ten at night. Right now the answer is
no. We know the phone is busy on Saturday mornings, and we know that some of you give up before we
pick up. We are sorry about that. For now, the book by the register is the system.
