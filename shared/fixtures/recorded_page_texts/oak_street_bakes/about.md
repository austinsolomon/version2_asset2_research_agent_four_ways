# About Oak Street Bakes

Source URL: https://oak-street-bakes.example/about
Recorded on: 2026-09-14

Oak Street Bakes is owned by Dale Pruitt, who opened the first shop in Hyde Park in 2011 and still
owns and runs both shops today. There are no investors and no partners, and there is no head office.
There is Dale, a kitchen crew of nine, and a counter crew of eleven across two shops.

## How it started

Dale Pruitt spent twelve years baking in hotel kitchens in Houston and Chicago, mostly overnight,
mostly for banquets that nobody remembered the bread from. In 2011 a former shoe repair shop in Hyde
Park came up for rent, with a back room just big enough for one deck oven. Dale signed the lease,
built the front counter out of an old bowling lane, and sold out of everything by 9:40 on the first
morning. The pecan blondie on the menu today is the same recipe from that first week.

The name comes from the live oak in front of the Hyde Park shop. There is no Oak Street in the
address, which confuses a delivery driver about once a month.

## Two shops, on purpose

We have two locations, and two is the plan.

- **Hyde Park**, 12 Example Street, Chicago, IL 60615. Opened in 2011. This is where the kitchen is,
  and everything we sell is baked here.
- **South Chicago**, 112 Example Street, Chicago, IL 60617. Opened in 2019. It has a counter, a coffee
  bar and a pickup shelf, and no oven. A van leaves Hyde Park at 5:45 every morning to stock it.

People ask about a third shop. The honest answer is that one kitchen can bake well for two counters
and not for three, and Dale would rather run two good shops than three tired ones.

## Why we moved ordering online

Until 2024, a cake order meant a phone call, a paper book and a fair amount of hope. On Saturday
mornings the phone rang while the line went out the door, and whoever answered it was the person who
should have been boxing croissants. In the spring of 2024 Dale turned on the online store that came
with the registers, put every item in it, and stopped taking orders by phone.

It was the best change we have made since the second oven. About six orders in ten now come through
the [online store](https://oak-street-bakes.example/order). Customers choose their own pickup time and
pay ahead, the kitchen sees tomorrow's cakes on one screen, and nobody has to read anybody's
handwriting. The Saturday line is shorter, and the people in it are in a better mood.

## The people

  between the two shops.
- **Marisol Peña, kitchen lead.** Has run the overnight bake since 2015.
- **Curtis Boone, South Chicago shop lead.** Opened the second shop in 2019 and has run its counter
  ever since.

## What we care about

Flour from a Illinois mill, butter with nothing in it but cream, and fruit from whoever is growing the
good fruit that month. We pay the whole crew above the going rate, and we close both shops for a week
in August so that everyone gets a real break at the same time.

Whatever is left at closing goes to a neighborhood food pantry, and the day-old loaves become the
bread pudding you see on Sundays.
