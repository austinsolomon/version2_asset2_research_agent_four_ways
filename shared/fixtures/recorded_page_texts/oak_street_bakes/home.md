# Oak Street Bakes — bread, pastry and cakes in Hyde Park and South Chicago

Source URL: https://oak-street-bakes.example/
Recorded on: 2026-09-14

**[Order Online](https://oak-street-bakes.example/order)** — order and pay ahead, then skip the line

Oak Street Bakes is a neighborhood bakery with two shops in Chicago. We have been baking in Hyde Park
since 2011 and in South Chicago since 2019. Everything in both cases is mixed, shaped and baked in the
Hyde Park kitchen before the sun comes up, and a van carries the South Chicago half across the river
by 6:30 every morning.

## Order ahead, pick up when it suits you

Most of our regulars no longer wait in the Saturday line. They open the
[online store](https://oak-street-bakes.example/order), fill a cart, choose a shop and a pickup time,
and pay by card. The box is on the pickup shelf with their name on it when they walk in. The store is
open around the clock, so you can order Sunday's croissants from the couch on Thursday night.

You can order for today until 11am, as long as the item is still in stock, and up to fourteen days
ahead. Whole cakes need three days.

## What we bake

**Bread.** A sourdough country loaf, a honey whole wheat sandwich loaf, a jalapeño cheddar loaf on
Friday and Saturday, and baguettes that come out at 7am and again at 1pm.

**Pastry.** Butter croissants, almond croissants, pecan sticky buns, fruit danishes that follow the
season, and a kolache of the week, because this is Illinois.

**Cookies and bars.** Brown butter chocolate chip, oatmeal and dried cherry, lemon bars, and a salted
pecan blondie that has been on the menu since the first week.

**Cakes.** Six-inch and eight-inch layer cakes in five flavors, plus a Illinois sheet cake that feeds
twenty. Cakes are ordered through the online store like everything else. Pick a flavor, type the
message you want piped on top, and choose a pickup day at least three days out.

**Coffee.** Drip, cold brew and espresso drinks at both shops. Coffee is the one thing we do not sell
ahead. We make it when you walk in.

## Catering boxes

Pastry boxes for a dozen or for fifty, sandwich loaves sliced for a crowd, and cookie trays. All of
them are in the online store under Boxes and trays, with the notice each one needs written next to it.

## News

We moved all ordering online in the spring of 2024. Before that, cake orders lived in a paper book and
on the answering machine, and we lost one order a month to bad handwriting. We have not lost one
since. If you would still rather talk to a person, come by either shop and we will place the order
with you on the tablet at the counter.
