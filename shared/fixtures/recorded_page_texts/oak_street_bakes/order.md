## Step 4 — Check out

Enter your name, email and mobile number, then pay by card. We take all major credit and debit cards
and the wallet on your phone. Payment is taken in full when you place the order. There is nothing to
pay at the counter. We will text you when the order is on the pickup shelf.

## Changing or canceling an order

Use the link in your receipt email to change a pickup time or cancel. Orders canceled at least 24
hours before pickup are refunded to the same card in full. Cakes can be changed up to 48 hours before
pickup.

## How this store works

The store runs on the same point-of-sale system as the registers in both shops. An order placed here
prints in the Hyde Park kitchen the moment you pay, and it comes off the same stock count the
counter uses. We do not take cake or box orders over the phone any more. If you call 312-555-0103
with an order, we will kindly point you back to this page, or place it with you on the counter tablet
if you are standing in the shop.

We deliver nothing ourselves and we are not listed on any delivery app. Every order is for pickup.
