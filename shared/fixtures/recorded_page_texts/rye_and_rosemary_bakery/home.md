Rye & Rosemary is a sourdough and pastry bakery in the Roscoe Village part of west Chicago. Everything in
the case was mixed, shaped and baked in the room you can see through the window behind the counter.
We start the ovens at 3am and we open the door at 7am.

## Hours

Tuesday to Saturday, 7am to 3pm. Sunday, 8am to 1pm. Closed Monday. We close for the week of
Thanksgiving after the Wednesday pickup rush, and for the first ten days of August, when it is too hot
for the dough and for us.

## Where to find us

27 Example Street, Chicago, IL 60657. We are the brick storefront with the green door and the rosemary
bushes in the planters, next to the dry cleaner. There are four parking spaces in front and more on
the side street. The shop has a standing bar along the window and two benches outside; it is a place
to pick up bread more than a place to sit.

Phone: 312-555-0118, answered during shop hours.

## What we bake

**Sourdough.** The house loaf is a rye and rosemary sourdough, which is where the name comes from:
thirty percent whole rye, fresh rosemary from the planters out front, and a two-day ferment. We also
bake a plain country sourdough, a seeded whole wheat, and an olive and lemon loaf on Friday and
Saturday. About ninety loaves on a weekday and a hundred and sixty on a Saturday.

**Pastry.** Butter croissants, almond croissants, a ham and gruyere croissant, a rosemary and sea salt
twist, and a cinnamon morning bun. The laminated dough takes three days from start to finish, so the
count is fixed by Wednesday and we cannot make more on the day.

**Sweets.** A brown butter chocolate chip cookie, a rye brownie, a lemon and rosemary shortbread, and
one seasonal tart. In September the tart is fig. In spring it is strawberry.

**Focaccia.** Rosemary focaccia by the slab, plain or with tomato, from 11am. It is usually gone by
1pm.

**Coffee.** Drip coffee and cold brew only. We are a bakery that pours coffee, not a coffee shop that
sells pastry, and there is no espresso machine.

## Order Ahead

Most of what we bake sells out, and on Saturday it sells out early. If you want to be certain of a
loaf, a box of croissants or a slab of focaccia for a dinner, use Order Ahead and we will hold it for
you. Holiday pies and dinner rolls for Thanksgiving are Order Ahead only, and the list opens on the

## The people

Rye & Rosemary is a small crew: Sofia at the oven, two bakers on the bench with her, and three people
who take turns at the counter. There is more about how the bakery started, and who runs it, on the
[About](https://rye-and-rosemary.example/about) page.

## Good to know

- We bake with wheat, rye, butter, eggs, milk and tree nuts in one small room. We cannot promise that
  anything we sell has not touched any of them.
- Day-old bread is half price from a basket by the door, while it lasts.
- We take cards and cash at the register.
- Dogs are welcome on the benches outside, and there is a water bowl by the green door.
