# Order Ahead — Rye & Rosemary Bakery

Source URL: https://rye-and-rosemary.example/order
Recorded on: 2026-09-14

Order Ahead means we set your bread and pastry aside before the door opens, so it cannot sell out
from under you. Here is how it works.

## How to order ahead

Order ahead by calling the shop or emailing our order form. We confirm by phone. Pay when you pick up.

That is the whole system, and we would rather tell you now than have you hunt for a button. You cannot
place an order on this website. There is no shopping cart, no checkout and no card payment here, and
we are not on any ordering app or delivery service. An order exists once one of us has phoned you back
and read it out to you.

- **By phone:** call 312-555-0118 during shop hours, Tuesday to Saturday 7am to 3pm and Sunday 8am to
  1pm. Whoever picks up writes your order on the clipboard by the register.
- **By email:** copy the order form at the bottom of this page into an email, fill it in, and send it
  to orders@rye-and-rosemary.example. You can also print it, fill it in by hand and drop it at the
  counter. We read the order inbox once a day, at about 2pm, after the lunch rush.

Either way, we confirm by phone. If you emailed the form, expect a call from 312-555-0118 by the end
of the next open day. If we cannot reach you after two tries we do not bake the order, so please give
us a number you answer.

## How much notice we need

- Bread, croissants and focaccia: two days. The dough for Saturday is mixed on Thursday.
- More than twenty of any one thing: five days.
- Thanksgiving pies and dinner rolls: the list opens on the first of November and closes when it is
  full, which last year took nine days.

Emails that arrive after 2pm count as the next day. If you need something for tomorrow, do not email.
Call, and we will tell you honestly what is possible.

## What you can order ahead

- Rye and rosemary sourdough, country sourdough, seeded whole wheat — $11 a loaf
- Olive and lemon loaf, Friday and Saturday only — $12
- Butter croissants — $4.50 each, or $24 for a box of six
- Almond croissants and ham and gruyere croissants — $5.75 each
- Rosemary focaccia, half slab (feeds six) — $18; full slab (feeds twelve) — $34
- Cookies and rye brownies by the dozen — $36
- Seasonal tart, nine inch — $38

We do not make custom cakes, and we do not deliver or ship. Pickup is at the shop, 27 Example Street,
Chicago, IL 60657.

## Paying and picking up

Pay when you pick up, at the register, by card or cash. We do not take deposits, and nobody here will
ever ask for a card number over the phone or by email. Your order waits on the shelf behind the
register with your name on it. Please collect it by noon; after noon on a busy day we may sell it to
the person standing in front of us.

If your plans change, call us the day before and there is no charge.

## Why it works this way

We know what an Order Ahead button usually means, and we know ours lands on a phone number and a form.
We are bakers, and the clipboard has not failed us yet. It does have limits. The inbox gets read once
a day. The phone goes unanswered when all three of us at the counter are bagging bread. On the
Saturday before Thanksgiving last year we missed eleven calls in one morning. If you have given up on
reaching us before, we are sorry, and we would still like to bake for you.
